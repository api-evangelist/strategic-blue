# Strategic Blue

> Strategic Blue is a UK-headquartered cloud FinOps company specialising in AWS rate optimization through commitment management. Founded in 2010 by Dr James Mitchell, a former commodities trader, Strategic Blue delivers Reserved Instance and Savings Plan savings of up to 40% without lock-in, forecasting requirements, or technical compromise.

Strategic Blue treats cloud like a commodity, applying price risk management techniques from financial markets to optimize AWS commitment discounts. The company serves customers across commercial and public sectors, with particular strength in FinTech, HealthTech, and Science/Research organisations.

Key differentiators:
- 14+ years of cloud cost forecasting and rate optimization expertise
- Commodity trading background enables sophisticated commitment management
- No technical access required — only cost and usage metadata (least-privilege)
- No impact on existing AWS relationships, invoicing, or technical operations
- Secure-by-design with ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 27001 certifications

## Services

- [Automate](https://strategic-blue.com/automate-business): Rate optimization for commercial enterprises. Strategic Blue manages Reserved Instances and Savings Plans, taking a percentage share of commitment-derived savings. Delivers up to 45% savings with no lock-in contracts and full technical flexibility.
- [Accelerate (Public Sector)](https://strategic-blue.com/accelerate): AWS resale with rate optimization for UK public sector organisations at no service fee. Provides a perfect discount with no risk of under or overspending and no usage commitments.
- [Savings Review](https://portal.strategic-blue.com/#/sign-up): Free personalised savings projection based on your actual AWS cost and usage data.
